Team & Student Skill Dashboard: Institute for Entrepreneurship 2025

Welcome to the Institute for Entrepreneurship (IFE) 2025 Skill Development Dashboard. This interactive report visualizes how students and teams developed across six core skill categories throughout the five-week fellowship. Each chart provides a snapshot of growth from Pre-Program → Mid-Program → Post-Program, illustrating both individual and team-level progress.

How to Read This Dashboard

You can explore:

  • Team Assessments: Aggregate skill trends and collective growth for each of the six IFE teams.
  • Student Assessments: Detailed breakdowns of self-assessed and overall (peer + performance-based) skill development for each participant.

Understanding the Charts

Each chart is a radar graph that highlights performance across the six core skill areas:

  • Adaptability & Creativity – personal growth, innovation, and flexibility.
  • Problem-Solving & Critical Thinking – analytical reasoning and decision-making skills.
  • Emotional Intelligence & Ethical Thinking – empathy, integrity, and self-awareness.
  • Collaboration & Communication – teamwork, leadership, and clarity in expression.
  • Technical Aptitude – comfort with tools, technology, and data-driven work.
  • Time Management – organization, reliability, and ability to prioritize effectively.

Scores range from 1 to 5 and represent demonstrated skill growth, not fixed judgments of ability. A 1 indicates minimal evidence of that skill at the given checkpoint, while a 5 indicates consistent demonstration and peer recognition of that strength.

How Scores Are Calculated

Each skill rating is derived from multiple data sources collected throughout the program:

  • Student Self-Assessments: Reflections where students evaluate their own progress and mindset.
  • Peer & Team Evaluations: Feedback from teammates regarding collaboration, reliability, and performance.
  • Journey Platform Analytics: Engagement and completion data from weekly tasks and reflections.
  • Final Pitch Assessments: Scores reflecting communication, innovation, and applied teamwork during presentations.

The combination of these measures yields two complementary perspectives:

  • Self-Assessment: How students perceive their own growth.
  • Overall Assessment: A holistic view integrating peer feedback and measurable performance.

How to Navigate

  • Use the team tabs across the top to switch between the six IFE teams.
  • Within each team, select a student tab to view individual progress.
  • Toggle between views (Overall vs Self, Overall Only, or Self Only) to compare perspectives.
  • Hover or tap on any point in the radar charts to see precise values.
  • You can toggle specific lines on or off for a clearer comparison between stages.
